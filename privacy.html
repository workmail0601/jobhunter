Privacy Policy

How StemNGO Jobs Kenya collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated:

StemNGO Jobs Kenya ("StemNGO Jobs", "we", "us", "our") operates the website www.stemngo.co.ke, a job board connecting STEM and NGO/development-sector jobseekers with recruiters in Kenya. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By using this site, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information we collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Information you provide directly — such as your name, email address, phone number, CV/resume content, cover letters, and any other details you submit through our contact form, CV service requests, or job postings (for recruiters). These submissions are handled through our embedded Google Form.
  • Job application data — when you apply to a listing, we may forward relevant details to the employer or route you to the employer's own application system, which is subject to that employer's own privacy practices.
  • Automatically collected information — such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and referral source, collected through standard web logs and cookies.
  • Cookies and similar technologies — used for site functionality and by third-party services described in Section 3 below (e.g. advertising and analytics).

2. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate, maintain and improve the job board and CV services;
  • Publish job listings and connect recruiters with candidates;
  • Respond to enquiries submitted through our contact form;
  • Send updates you have opted into, such as new job alerts or career guides;
  • Monitor site usage and detect misuse, fraud or abuse; and
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Third-party services

We rely on a small number of third-party services to operate this site, each of which processes data under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Forms — powers our contact and submission forms.
  • Google AdSense — displays advertisements on this site and may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits here and other sites. You can manage ad personalisation through Google Ads Settings.
  • Google Sheets / Apps Script — used behind the scenes to store and serve job listing data.

4. Sharing with recruiters and employers

When you apply for a job through a listing on this site, information you choose to submit (such as your CV) may be shared with the recruiter or employer named in that listing, or you may be redirected to an external application page they control. We are not responsible for how third-party employers or recruiters handle information you submit directly to them.

5. Data retention

We retain contact form submissions and job-related enquiries for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to them and to maintain records of recruiter and jobseeker interactions, after which they are deleted or anonymised.

6. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, contact us using the details in Section 8. We will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Children's privacy

This site is intended for jobseekers and recruiters and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18.

8. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please reach out via our contact form.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
